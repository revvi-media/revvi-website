Frequently asked questions

Does automation replace staff?

No. It removes repetitive work so your team can focus on higher-value tasks.

Do we need new software?

Not necessarily. Revvi is designed to work with the systems you already use wherever practical.

Do staff need to learn anything new?

Most automations run in the background. Your team can keep using their existing systems and simply review and approve work where required.

Is everything automatic?

Only if you want it to be. Many clients choose a review-and-approve workflow for anything important.
