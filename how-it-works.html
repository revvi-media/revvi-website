The 5 steps

  1. 1

    Assess

    We learn how your team works, what systems you use and where repetitive tasks are costing you time or money.

  2. 2

    Recommend

    We identify what can realistically be automated, estimate the potential value and recommend where to start.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build the automation around your existing systems and workflows. No unnecessary new software or complicated processes.

  4. 4

    Test & Approve

    We test everything before launch. Your team reviews the workflow and human approval can remain in place wherever it's needed.

  5. 5

    Support

    We monitor and maintain your automation, make improvements and help identify the next opportunity when you're ready.

You stay in control, human approval built into every step.
