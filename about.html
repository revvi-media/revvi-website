Business experience. Technical capability. Practical automation.

Revvi is an Australian business based in Queensland. We combine practical business experience with technical automation capability to fix the processes costing businesses time, money and opportunities.

Meet the founder

Mark Ferguson

Mark brings two decades of experience building and working with Australian businesses, with a practical understanding of the repetitive processes that consume teams' time. He founded Revvi to make practical automation accessible without forcing companies to replace the systems they already use.

Our approach

Automate the work. Keep the human judgement.

Every business has repetitive work that pulls staff away from more valuable tasks. Revvi connects to the software you already use and automates those repetitive processes, drafting emails, preparing documents, updating CRM records and generating reports. Your staff simply review and approve where required, then keep working in the systems they already know.

Why Revvi

Good people shouldn't spend their day on repetitive work.

We saw skilled staff spending hours every week on tasks that didn't need a human doing them manually, while the technology to improve those processes was often already available but underused. Revvi exists to close that gap.
